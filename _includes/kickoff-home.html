02 / PHYSICAL SELF-PLAY

Learning
through self-play.

SKILD AI · HUMANOID SOCCER

I independently developed Skild AI’s humanoid soccer demo, training policies through self-play in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and transferring them to real hardware.

With a scoring objective and evolving opponents, dribbling, shielding, and tackling emerged without handcrafted rewards for these behaviors. After 140 years of simulated play, we deployed the policy on a real humanoid.

SKILD AI · ROBOT PARKOUR

Agile whole-body control on real hardware: a humanoid robot traversing obstacles, jumping onto boxes, and moving through the world.

I lead Unitree G1 sim-to-real deployment, including simulation fidelity, domain randomization, and control tuning, and built the parkour deployment pipeline and safety filters for latency, sensor noise, and hardware constraints.
